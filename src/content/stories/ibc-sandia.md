**Advised America's largest national lab on career paths at 19.**

- **Situation:** Sandia's project-management center had roles no one could clearly define, and promotions stopped at PM.
- **What I did:** Built competency models for the two core roles and a career roadmap. Benchmarked 10 defense orgs.
- **Value:** Four deliverables handed to Sandia HR. The client flew us to Albuquerque to present them.
- **What I learned:** Most people there couldn't clearly say what their role required. That was the actual problem we were fixing.
