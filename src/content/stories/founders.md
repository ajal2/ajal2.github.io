**Treasurer and board member of Founders, UIUC's student entrepreneurship club.**

- **Situation:** Founders (Illinois Entrepreneurs) is UIUC's flagship student entrepreneurship org. It runs 54, a three-day startup weekend, and Forge, a student-run entrepreneurship conference.
- **What I did:** Served on the university incubator's finance team, which funded its active student startups, and managed the org's ~$5,000 semester events budget.
- **Value:** Startups got their funding, events ran on budget, and I won the 54 pitch competition.
- **What I learned:** At 19, my job was asking founders to walk me through their numbers before we funded them.
