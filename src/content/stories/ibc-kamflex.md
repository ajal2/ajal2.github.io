**Helped a conveyor manufacturer decide to become a data company.**

- **Situation:** Kamflex’s customers' real problem was downtime on conveyors they already owned, not buying new ones.
- **What we did:** Designed a predictive-maintenance service, from sensors to AI, with a vendor benchmark, cost model, staffing plan, and pricing.
- **Value:** A full launch plan, supported with real examples (Coca-Cola's digital twin, Harley's $200M) to make the business case.
- **What I learned:** Small-Mid sized players usually don’t buy a data product from a pitch. They buy someone else's proof.
