**Selected as 1 of 25 for a funded week in Silicon Valley, at 20.**

- **Situation:** Every January, Grainger Engineering funds a week in Silicon Valley for ~25 students. Selection took two interviews.
- **What I did:** Met with Tom Siebel at C3 AI, Jawed Karim of YouTube, and teams at Y Combinator, Menlo Ventures, Scale AI, Intel, and Impossible Foods.
- **Value:** Saw how founders and investors evaluate ideas. Every pitch we watched came down to whether you could put a number on the value.
- **What I learned:** Two of the people we met started out as Illinois students. That stuck with me.
