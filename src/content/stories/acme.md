**Won Best Paper for a cost-variance analysis at a precision machine shop.**

- **Situation:** A precision CNC shop in Chicago’s suburbs, with 85 machines, making aerospace and defense parts, running on a 1990s ERP and month-old Excel reports.
- **What we did:** My own piece was the economics: I sized $1.44M a year in cost variance and made the investment case. The team built the Power BI dashboard on the legacy ERP.
- **Value:** Daily variance visibility instead of month-old reports, plus Best Paper at a 41-team national design competition.
- **What I learned:** The shop already had the data it needed. The problem was that nobody saw it until a month later.
