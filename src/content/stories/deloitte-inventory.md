**Audited a manufacturing plant's inventory and found nearly half of it had not moved in three months.**

- **Situation:** The plant had taken in a large stock transfer and never sorted it. Nobody could say how long any item had been sitting.
- **What I did:** Pulled stock on hand and goods-movement history from SAP, aged every material line by its last movement date, then coordinated a physical count to check the books against the floor.
- **Value:** 45% of stock value was idle for 90 days or more, and buying was still going on against it. Management had a number to act on, and committed to rack labelling and bin mapping with a named owner and a date.
- **What I learned:** The data was already sitting in SAP. Nobody had subtracted the dates.
On the dispatch side, the purchase-order tracker had no field for a fulfilment date, so nobody could tell how long an order had been open. Over two hundred were waiting.

Two caveats I would raise before anyone else does. The stock came in from another site, so the movement dates predate this plant and the true age is probably older than what I could measure. And the physical count was scoped to three days, which was not enough to get through everything.
